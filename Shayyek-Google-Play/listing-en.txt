=== App name (max 30) ===
Shayyek: Auto Replies & DMs

=== Short description (max 80) ===
Auto-reply to Instagram comments & story replies, and send the link by DM.

=== Full description ===
Shayyek replies to your Instagram comments and story replies automatically, and sends your link, discount code or menu to the customer's DMs within seconds — so no "how much?" or "where's the link?" goes unanswered.

Built for online stores and content creators in Saudi Arabia. Shayyek works only through Meta's official Instagram API and never asks for your Instagram password.

HOW IT WORKS
1. A follower comments on your post or reel, or replies to your story, with a keyword you choose.
2. Shayyek replies publicly and sends the message and link in a private message.
3. You follow the results: comments, delivered messages and link clicks.

FEATURES
• Auto-reply campaigns for comments, story replies and story mentions
• Private messages with links or codes, buttons and interactive menus
• One inbox for your conversations, with labels and saved replies
• Fair giveaways from comments, with a draw video and a winner card
• Schedule posts, reels and stories, with best-time suggestions
• Campaign and post analytics, with link click tracking
• Connect your Salla store to see orders and sales from each campaign
• Instant push notifications for what matters
• Staff accounts with limited permissions, and two-step verification

OFFICIAL AND SAFE
• Uses Meta's official Instagram APIs only
• No Instagram password — you connect through Meta's own login page
• Follows Meta's messaging rules to keep your account safe

REQUIREMENTS
• An Instagram professional account (Business or Creator) — switching is free in Instagram settings
• A Shayyek account (a free plan is available)
