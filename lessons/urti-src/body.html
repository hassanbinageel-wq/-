Upper respiratory tract infections (URTIs)

URTIs are common infections in children affecting the nose, pharynx and tonsils.

The common types include

Most cases are caused by viruses.

Common cold · definition & causes

Common cold is a viral infection of the upper respiratory tract, mainly affecting the nose and throat.

Common cold · physical examination

General examination

  • Child may appear mildly ill.
  • Normal or mildly elevated temperature.

Nose examination

  • Nasal congestion.
  • Redness of nasal mucosa.
  • Watery nasal discharge.

Throat examination

  • Mild redness of the pharynx.
  • Tonsils usually normal.

Chest examination

  • Usually normal breath sounds.

Common cold · management & nursing care

Management

Adequate fluids
Rest
Saline nasal drops
Paracetamol for fever when needed
Honey for cough in children older than one year

Nursing care

  1. Monitor temperature and respiratory rate.
  2. Encourage fluid intake.
  3. Keep the nose clean.
  4. Provide adequate nutrition.
  5. Educate parents about handwashing.
Note: Antibiotics are not indicated for common cold.

Pharyngitis · definition & causes

Pharyngitis is inflammation of the pharynx (throat), causing pain and difficulty swallowing.

Causes

Viral

Adenovirus
Influenza virus
Rhinovirus

Bacterial

Group A Streptococcus (GAS)

General examination

  • Child may appear ill.
  • Fever may be present.

Throat examination

  • Redness of posterior pharyngeal wall.
  • Pharyngeal swelling.
  • Possible white patches or exudates.

Neck examination

  • Enlarged cervical lymph nodes.
  • Tender anterior cervical lymph nodes, especially in bacterial infection.

Viral pharyngitis

Fluids

Bacterial pharyngitis

Antibiotics such as Penicillin or Amoxicillin when indicated.

Important: Antibiotics do not treat viral infections.

Tonsillitis · definition & causes

Tonsillitis is inflammation of the palatine tonsils, located on both sides of the throat.

Causes

Viral infections

most common

Bacterial infections

especially Group A Streptococcus

Tonsillitis · clinical manifestations

Sore throat
Fever
Difficulty swallowing
Enlarged tonsils
White or yellow patches on tonsils
Bad breath
Enlarged cervical lymph nodes
Decreased appetite

In young children

Refusal to eat or drink
Vomiting
Irritability

General examination

  • Fever.
  • Child may appear sick and uncomfortable.

Mouth & throat examination

  • Enlarged tonsils.
  • Red and swollen tonsils.
  • White or yellowish exudates may be present.
  • Severe cases: tonsils nearly touching each other.

Neck examination

  • Enlarged and tender anterior cervical lymph nodes.

Comparison between the three conditions

Common cold

Mainly affects the nose
  • Runny nose and sneezing.
  • Mild fever.
  • Normal tonsils usually.

Pharyngitis

Affects the pharynx
  • Redness of posterior pharyngeal wall.
  • Sore throat and painful swallowing.

Tonsillitis

Affects the tonsils
  • Enlarged and red tonsils.
  • Possible tonsillar exudates.
  • Fever and difficulty swallowing.
